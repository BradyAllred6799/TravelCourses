# Email Feedback

Good morning Brady,

There is a lot of really good content here, and they are incredibly thorough. Most of my feedback falls into two buckets: structure/learner experience and content.

## Structure / Learner Experience

Overall, I think both courses would benefit from some simplification and a little more intentional organization.
There are several places where the content is very word-heavy. I would like us to keep the main learning path focused on what the learner actually needs to do, then use collapsible tabs, or other reference-style interactions for exceptions and additional detail.
I would also revisit the order and grouping of some topics. In the Expense Report course especially, Expense Item guidance is scattered throughout the course. I think it may be easier to follow if we group those together more intentionally, potentially separating common Expense Items from complex/special Expense Items.
I would like us to be mindful of technical/system language where simpler learner language would work. Terms like configuration, pre-built rows, or other implementation-heavy language are not always necessary for the learner to complete the process.
Where we do have step-based content, I think carousels work well. Reference information can be collapsible, and clickable screenshots are helpful when the learner actually needs field-level guidance. For some of the more text-heavy, less interactive sections, I would also like to explore optional audio playback so we are not relying entirely on large amounts of on-screen text.
Both courses also move between Employee as Self and On Behalf Of. I am okay with covering both, and honestly think that may be the better approach because the processes are so closely related, but I would like the framing to be more intentional and consistent.
Longer term, I also think it may be worth discussing whether Spend Authorization and Expense Report training could ultimately live within a larger, well-indexed Traveling at UF course. There is so much overlap across the travel lifecycle that connecting the pieces may help reduce repetition and make the overall process easier to follow.

## Content

There are a few areas where I think we could strengthen the actual content. I think these courses would be stronger if learners came in with a basic understanding of worktags. The travel courses can then focus on how worktags are used within the travel process rather than teaching the broader worktag structure from scratch.
I would also like to include more context around booking travel. Even though booking happens outside of Workday, booking decisions come back into the Expense Report through booking method, exceptions, card transactions, receipts, and related fields.
Once we have a clearer picture of what is changing versus staying the same in the UF Travel Directives, I would like to add more direct references back to them throughout both courses. There are several places where it is important to distinguish between what Workday enforces and what UF policy requires, and I think tying that guidance back to the Directives will make the training stronger and easier to maintain.
I also think we could strengthen the guidance around repayment processes, particularly for outstanding cash advance balances and accidental personal use of a UF card. Right now, we explain when money is owed, but I think learners would benefit from clearer direction on what happens next and where they should go for repayment instructions.
Finally, I think we could probably streamline some of the recap content. In a few places we have a summary, another table, a checklist, and then a wrap-up covering similar concepts. I think relying on one strong final recap or checklist would help keep the end of the courses lighter and more focused.
