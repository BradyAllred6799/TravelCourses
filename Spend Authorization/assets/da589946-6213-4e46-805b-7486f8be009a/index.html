Explore Roles by Process Function

A person may hold more than one role, but initiating, reviewing, and approving remain separate functions.

How to use this

  • Select a tab to show the roles for that part of the process. The Initiate tab is open when the page loads.
  • Select a role tile to expand its details, then select it again to collapse it. The plus sign changes to a minus sign while a tile is open.
  • Using a keyboard, press Tab to reach the tab list, then press the Left or Right arrow key to move between tabs. Press Tab to reach a role tile, then press Enter or the Space bar to open or close it.
  • Printing the page opens every role tile and includes all three tabs.

Initiate

Initiators create, prepare, save, modify, and submit Spend Authorizations. Initiating a transaction does not provide approval authority.

Employee as Self

Creates a request for their own travel

✓ Initiator responsibilities

  • ✓ Create and submit a Spend Authorization for their own travel.
  • ✓ Modify or save their request for later.
  • ✓ Verify the position, company, travel dates, description, Business Purpose, and Justification.
  • ✓ Enter accurate estimates, travel lines, worktags, destinations, attendees, and supporting information.
  • ✓ Correct errors and review alerts before submitting.

Expense Data Entry Specialist

EDES · Creates requests on behalf of others

✓ Initiator responsibilities

  • ✓ Create, prepare, modify, save, and submit Spend Authorizations for workers in the same Workday company.
  • ✓ For a guest or student whom UF will reimburse directly, first submit a Create External Committee Member (ECM) Request for Guest Travel through Create Request. Include the person’s contact details, ACH contact preference, supporting documentation, and comments. After the non-worker is established in Workday, use Create Spend Authorization for Non-Worker and manually add the payee to Attendees on every applicable line. Because an ECM has no My Tasks inbox, Workday position, or Manager, the request has no traveler-review or Manager step and routes first to the Expenses Cost Center Coordinator.
  • ✓ Gather complete travel dates, destinations, estimated costs, attendees, funding, and cash-advance information.
  • ✓ Verify header information, lines, worktags, Item Details, attachments, errors, and alerts before submitting.
  • ✓ View the Find Spend Authorizations, Find Expense Reports, and Find Credit Card Transactions reports.

Important: The EDES role has no approval authority. An EDES who approves does so through a separate approval role.

Review

Reviewers verify that the request is complete and accurate. These reviewers may edit the Spend Authorization, save unfinished changes for later, or send it back when corrections are required.

Employee as Self

Reviews an international request created on their behalf

✓ What the employee reviews

  • ✓ Travel dates.
  • ✓ Business Purpose and Justification.
  • ✓ How the trip supports UF’s mission.
  • ✓ Destination and estimated amount on each line.
  • ✓ Required supporting documentation.

Available actions: The employee may approve, edit, send back, add an approver, or save for later. The employee must personally complete the International Travel Attestation before the request routes to the Manager.

A domestic Spend Authorization created on an employee’s behalf does not route to that employee for review.

Expenses Cost Center Coordinator

Administrative and financial reviewer

✓ What the coordinator reviews

  • ✓ Whether the Spend Authorization or Expense Report is accurate and complete.
  • ✓ Worktags and other financial information.
  • ✓ Whether financial and compliance requirements are met.
  • ✓ Whether each UF employee listed in Attendees has a separate Spend Authorization.
  • ✓ Whether the lines, estimates, Budget Dates, memos, and supporting information are accurate.

Important: This role is assigned by cost center, so a coordinator sees only their assigned cost centers. The coordinator may edit a submitted request and use Save for Later to retain unfinished changes, but has no financial approval authority. Apart from the traveler acting as Employee as Self on an international request, other workflow approvers must use Send Back rather than edit.

Approve

Most approvers share the actions and restrictions below. Each role tile focuses on the information that approver is responsible for evaluating. Financial approval is determined for each line from its worktags and may route to a department-level Cost Center Manager, Grant Manager, Gift Manager, or Project Manager. Workday also evaluates whether specialized approval conditions apply; a step shown as not required reflects that evaluation rather than an oversight.

✓ Actions available to most approvers

  • Approve the request.
  • Send Back with a required comment explaining what must be corrected. A Financial Approver may send the request to the Expenses Cost Center Coordinator or to the request initiator.
  • Add Approvers when the request is correct but requires another qualified reviewer.
  • Cancel to exit without acting. The task remains in My Tasks.

× Restrictions and exceptions

  • Approvers cannot edit the Spend Authorization.
  • If information is incorrect, the approver must use Send Back.
  • No one may approve their own transaction.
  • The RISC Office and Expense Partner may also Deny in the circumstances described in their role tiles.

Manager

Required supervisory approval for every employee Spend Authorization.

Manager

Traveler’s HR supervisor of record

The Manager is the traveler’s HR supervisor of record, which in practice is the direct supervisor, not an HR generalist, HR liaison, or departmental HR manager. Manager approval is required on every employee Spend Authorization regardless of funding source, destination, or personal days.

What the Manager reviews

  • ✓ Whether the travel dates are accurate and reasonable.
  • ✓ Whether the Business Purpose and Justification explain who, what, where, when, why, and how the trip benefits UF.
  • ✓ Destinations and estimated amounts.
  • ✓ Whether the worktags are appropriate.
  • ✓ Whether a cash advance is necessary and properly justified.
  • ✓ Required supporting documentation.
  • ✓ Whether UF employees listed in Attendees have separate Spend Authorizations.

The Manager cannot edit the request. Use Send Back when a correction is required. Use Add Approvers, shown in some tasks as Approve and Send to Approvers, only when the request is correct but needs another qualified reviewer. That action records the Manager’s approval while routing the request.

RISC Office

International travel compliance approval.

RISC Office

International travel compliance approver

What the RISC Office reviews

  • ✓ Compliance with university international travel rules.
  • ✓ Compliance with sponsor requirements related to international travel.
  • ✓ Whether the destination is safe and approved.

Deny exception: The RISC Office is one of the limited roles authorized to deny a Spend Authorization. The Expense Partner may also deny a request containing a cash advance. Denial permanently ends the request, so a new Spend Authorization must be created.

Financial Approvers

Workday routes each line according to its funding worktags. Each card identifies the corresponding alternate approver when one is specified.

Cost Center Manager

Financial approver for Designated funding · Alternate approver: College/Unit Finance Leader

What the Cost Center Manager reviews

  • ✓ Whether the Designated worktag is an appropriate funding source.
  • ✓ Whether the applicable budget is open.
  • ✓ Whether sufficient funding is available.
  • ✓ Whether the related financial worktags align with the funding source.

Grant Manager

Financial approver for Grant funding · Alternate approver: Sponsored Programs Manager

What the Grant Manager reviews

  • ✓ Whether the travel is appropriately assigned to the Grant.
  • ✓ Whether the award and budget are open.
  • ✓ Whether sufficient funding is available.
  • ✓ Whether the expense is allowable under sponsor and award requirements.
  • ✓ Whether federally funded airfare meets applicable requirements when relevant.

Gift Manager

Financial approver for Gift funding · Alternate approver: Dean Gift Manager

What the Gift Manager reviews

  • ✓ Whether the travel is appropriately assigned to the Gift.
  • ✓ Whether the expense aligns with the donor-established purpose.
  • ✓ Whether the applicable budget is open.
  • ✓ Whether sufficient funding is available.

Project Manager

Financial approver for Project funding · Alternate approver: Not specified in this guide

What the Project Manager reviews

  • ✓ Whether the travel supports the Project’s defined purpose.
  • ✓ Whether the expense occurs within the Project’s established period.
  • ✓ Whether the Project and populated worktags are appropriate.
  • ✓ Whether the applicable budget is open.
  • ✓ Whether sufficient funding is available.

Specialized Approvers

Workday adds these approval steps automatically when the request meets the applicable condition.

C&G Accountant

Specialized award-line timing approval

What the C&G Accountant reviews

  • ✓ The Budget Date that triggered specialized approval.
  • ✓ The relationship between the Budget Date and Award Line End Date.
  • ✓ Whether the expense meets applicable award and timing requirements.

This approval is triggered when the Budget Date is within 90 days of the Award Line End Date.

CAS Approver

Cost Accounting Standards approval

What the CAS Approver reviews

  • ✓ The grant conditions that triggered CAS approval.
  • ✓ The account information associated with the expense.
  • ✓ Whether the proposed expense meets applicable Cost Accounting Standards requirements.

Construction Project Accountant

Specialized construction-funding approval

What the accountant reviews

  • ✓ The construction project funding that triggered the approval.
  • ✓ Whether the travel expense is appropriate for the construction project.
  • ✓ Whether applicable project and accounting requirements are met.

The same task may appear more than once. Approving one instance automatically clears the duplicates.

Expense Partner / Travel Office

Specialized cash-advance approval

What the Expense Partner reviews

  • ✓ The amount of the requested cash advance.
  • ✓ The Cash Advance Justification and why cash is necessary.
  • ✓ Whether the cash advance appears on its own separate line.
  • ✓ Whether the request was submitted no more than 30 days before the trip begins.
  • ✓ Whether the requested amount and funding information are appropriate.

Deny exception: The Expense Partner may deny a Spend Authorization containing a cash advance. Denial permanently ends the request.

What the Workflow Actions Mean

Select the action that matches whether the request is accurate, requires correction, or needs additional qualified review.

Submit
Routes a completed Spend Authorization into the approval process.
Save for Later
Retains unfinished entry or edits so the authorized participant can return later.
Approve
Completes the current review and routes the request to the next required step.
Send Back
Returns the request for correction. A comment explaining what must be corrected is required.
Add Approvers
Records the current approval and sends a correct request to another qualified reviewer.
Cancel Task
Exits an approval task without acting. The task remains in My Tasks.
Deny
Permanently ends the request. A denied Spend Authorization cannot be corrected and resubmitted.
